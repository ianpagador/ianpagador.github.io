My name is Ian Pagador, and I'm from Livermore, CA. I recently graduated from the University of California, Merced with a <b>B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering</b> and a <b>minor in Cognitive Science</b>, and I'm pursuing a career in <b>Data Analytics</b>.

I am currently a <b>Memory Developer Reviewer at Sunday Robotics</b>, where I ensure our multi-faceted humanoid robot Memo receives high quality data, helping him learn how to perform various household tasks. Also, I co-supervise teleoperations.

Previously, I worked as an <b>IT Contractor at the San Francisco Public Defender's Office</b>, designing Power Automate flows, building Python data pipelines with BeautifulSoup, PyMuPDF, PyMSSQL, and pandas, and automating case file migrations with PowerShell and Robocopy. I also completed a <b>capstone internship at BART</b>, developing a Faster R-CNN ResNet-50 milepost detection model in TensorFlow and integrating outputs into a Django backend for track visualization and PDF reporting.

I have experience across <b>Python, PowerShell, SQL, JavaScript/TypeScript, Bash, and R</b>, with tools and libraries including <b>Power Automate, Git, Flask, React, TensorFlow, OpenCV, pandas, and Selenium</b>. I've built machine learning models in <b>R Studio</b> and <b>Microsoft Azure</b>, and developed full-stack applications with <b>React, Flask, and SQLAlchemy</b>.

Beyond engineering, I've demonstrated open-mindedness, attention to detail, and leadership skills as both a section lead and executive board member for two on-campus dance organizations: <b>KAIROS and M4K at UC Merced</b>. There, I maintained audiovisual equipment, created and mastered audio mixes for performances, and constructed promotional graphics for events.
